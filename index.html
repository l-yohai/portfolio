About Me

I'm an AI Research Engineer at KakaoBank, focusing on developing finance-specific large language models (LLMs) and autonomous agents serving 26M+ active users.

My research addresses the fundamental challenges of making AI systems that truly emulate human cognitive processes, with particular emphasis on reasoning, memory, and alignment in real-world applications.

Human-like Reasoning

Developing parallel and continuous reasoning systems that enable models to "think before they speak" through soft token reasoning approaches.

Scalable Systems

Optimizing 200B+ parameter LLMs with interleaved reasoning, multi-instruction branching, and latency-optimized alignment for production environments.

Memory Architecture

Building PREMem - a next-generation memory system that consolidates experiences into structured knowledge for contextual learning.

Trustworthy AI

Ensuring consistency, transparency, calibrated outputs, reflective reasoning, and justifiable decision-making processes.
